Why cycle-aware support matters

Hormonal fluctuations across the cycle influence thermoregulation, metabolism, sleep quality, fluid balance, neuromuscular function, and symptom burden. A phase-aware framework supports stable planning across changing physiological conditions.

Supplement Information

A clinically styled overview of common micronutrients used in athlete stacks. Category pills link to available background sources when included.

Iron

Relevant to oxygen transport and endurance capacity. Considered when fatigue is present or when menstrual blood loss is higher. Monitoring iron status helps guide appropriate use.

  • Energy
  • Blood health
  • Lab-guided

Magnesium

Supports neuromuscular signaling and normal muscle contraction. Commonly included for recovery routines and symptom-focused support during higher-burden phases.

  • Recovery
  • Muscle
  • Sleep

Omega-3

A foundation component commonly used to support recovery and overall wellness. Often selected for consistent baseline coverage across phases.

  • Foundation
  • Recovery
  • Wellness

Vitamin D

Often selected based on baseline status. Commonly paired with training blocks that require long-term recovery consistency and foundational support.

  • Immune
  • Bone
  • Test-informed

Zinc

A conservative foundation option often used to support immune function. Total daily intake matters when stacking.

  • Immune
  • Foundation
  • Conservative

Electrolytes

Often emphasized during high-volume training periods. Supports hydration routines when sweat loss and training frequency increase.

  • Hydration
  • Load
  • Recovery
