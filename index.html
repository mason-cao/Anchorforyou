Why cycle-aware support matters

Training demand + cycle-related shifts can influence recovery, perceived energy, and day-to-day consistency. A structured approach helps you stay steady—especially during higher load weeks.

Supplement Information

A transparent overview of common components used in athlete-friendly stacks.

Iron

Often considered when fatigue or heavy bleeding is present. Best tailored to labs and tolerance.

  • Energy
  • Blood health
  • Lab-guided

Magnesium

Commonly used for muscle function and recovery support; sometimes included for cramps.

  • Recovery
  • Muscle
  • Wind-down

Omega-3

Often included as a general recovery foundation and wellness support.

  • Foundation
  • Recovery
  • Wellness

Vitamin D

Often guided by testing; dosing varies by individual.

  • Immune
  • Bone
  • Lab-guided

Zinc

Commonly included as a conservative foundation component—avoid excess long-term.

  • Immune
  • Foundation
  • Conservative

Electrolytes

Often used to support higher load weeks and hydration needs.

  • Hydration
  • Load
  • Recovery
