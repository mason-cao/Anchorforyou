Menstruation & supplement intake importance

Training demands + cycle-related shifts can increase micronutrient needs and influence recovery. A strategic, cycle-aligned approach helps support consistency, energy, and performance— while staying conservative and safety-focused.

Supplement Information

A transparent overview of common components used in athlete-friendly stacks. Final inclusion should be clinician-reviewed based on history, labs, and contraindications.

Iron (clinician guided)

Often relevant when fatigue or heavy bleeding is present. Should be tailored to labs and tolerance.

  • Energy support
  • Blood health
  • Lab-guided

Magnesium

Commonly used for muscle function and recovery support; sometimes used in cramp-focused stacks.

  • Recovery
  • Muscle
  • Sleep support*

Omega-3

Often included for general recovery support and overall wellness.

  • Recovery
  • Wellness
  • Foundation

Vitamin D

Frequently included when levels are low; best guided by testing and clinician review.

  • Immune support
  • Bone health
  • Lab-guided

Zinc

Often used as a conservative “foundation” component—avoid excess dosing long-term.

  • Immune
  • Foundation
  • Conservative

Electrolytes

Commonly used to support training demands—especially in heat or high volume weeks.

  • Hydration
  • Training load
  • Recovery

*Note: Individual responses vary. Always check interactions, allergies, and contraindications.
